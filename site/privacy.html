1Who we are

"You" means the dealer or shop owner who installs the app, and the staff who use it in the shop.

2What the app keeps

The app keeps what you enter or photograph to run your shop:

  • Shop details: name, address, GSTIN, phone, and licence numbers, dates and photos.
  • Stock: products, batches, expiry dates, godowns, purchase bills and their photos.
  • Sales: bills, credit notes, payments, and e-way bill numbers.
  • Customers: names, villages, phone numbers, credit (udhaar) balances and payments. For dealer customers it also keeps GSTIN, address and PIN code.
  • Crop photos and questions you ask for disease help.
  • The names and roles (owner or staff) of the phones paired with the shop computer.

3Where it is kept

  • On the shop computer. This is the main copy. The app also makes an automatic copy every 6 hours in a backups folder on the same computer, and optionally in an extra folder you choose, such as a pen drive.
  • On paired phones. Each phone keeps a copy so it works without Wi-Fi. Phones exchange data only with the shop computer, over the shop's Wi-Fi, after pairing with a one-time code.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you turn on Drive backup (section 5).

We do not run a server that stores or receives your shop records.

5Google user data (Drive backup)

Permission asked: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only. With this permission the app can see only the files it creates itself. It cannot see, open, list or change any other file in your Drive.

What we access

  • The folder "AgriKountr Backups" and the backup files (.kkb) that the app creates in it.
  • Your Google account email address, read from Drive's account information. The app shows it so you know which account holds your backups.

How we use it

  • To save a backup copy of your shop records each day and when you press "Back up now".
  • To list your backups and restore one when you ask, for example on a new computer.
  • To delete older backup copies the app made. It keeps the last 14, plus one per month for 12 months.

How it is stored and protected

  • Before upload, each backup is locked on your computer with AES-256-GCM encryption, using a key made from your backup PIN. The PIN never leaves your computer. Without it, nobody can open the backup: not Google, not us. If you forget the PIN, we cannot recover the backups.
  • The Google sign-in token and your email address are stored only on your shop computer, in the app's settings file. They are not sent to us or to any other service.
  • The backup leaves out the AI key and the e-way bill password.

Sharing

  • We do not receive your Google user data. We do not transfer it to anyone.
  • It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used for credit or lending decisions.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • No person at our side reads it.

AgriKountr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • In the app, go to Google Drive backup → More → Disconnect. This cancels the app's access with Google.
  • You can also remove AgriKountr at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To remove the backups, delete the "AgriKountr Backups" folder in your Drive.

6Your customers' details

Farmers' and buyers' names, phone numbers and credit balances belong to your shop's records. You decide what to record, and you are responsible for having a fair reason to keep it and for telling your customers, as Indian law requires, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The app keeps these details inside your shop and never sends them to us.

If a customer asks to see, correct or remove their details, you can do it in the app: open the customer and edit or delete.

7Phone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph bills, products, licences and crops.
  • Microphone: for voice typing, only after you tap the mic button.
  • Local network: to reach the shop computer on the shop Wi-Fi.
  • Files and sharing: to save Excel and portal files, and to share bills.

The app does not use your location or contacts, and it does not record in the background.

8Security

  • Phones pair with a 6-digit code that works for 10 minutes. Each phone then has its own key, and the owner can remove any phone.
  • Staff phones cannot delete licences or accounting entries.
  • Drive backups are locked with your PIN, as described in section 5.

No system is perfectly safe. Keep a password on the shop computer, and keep your backup PIN written in a safe place.

9How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Records stay until you delete them. GST law may require you to keep bills for several years, so check with your CA before deleting old bills.
  • To remove everything from the computer, delete the app's data folder (tray icon → Open data folder).
  • Uninstalling the app from a phone removes the phone's copy.
  • To remove Drive copies, delete the "AgriKountr Backups" folder in your Drive.
