కౌంటర్ కోసం తయారైంది · Made for the counter

Everything a village agri shop does in a day, in one app on the shop computer and the phones.

01

బిల్లు ఫోటో → స్టాక్Bill photo to stock

Photograph a company invoice and the items, batches, expiry dates and GST are read and checked: every line is multiplied, the total added up, and the GSTIN and the e-invoice QR verified.

02

GST బిల్లులుGST bills, like Tally

Tax invoice or bill of supply chosen automatically, IGST for other states, credit notes, and GSTR-1 and 3B summaries with an Excel file for your CA.

03

రైతు బాకీ ఖాతాUdhaar book for every farmer

Credit sales, part payments and balances by farmer and village. Bills go to the farmer on WhatsApp.

04

గోదాములు, కౌంటర్Godowns and counter stock

See stock in each godown and at the counter. The owner or staff picks which place each sale comes from.

05

డీలర్ అమ్మకాలు, ఈ-వే బిల్లుDealer sales and e-way bills

B2B sales to retailers with dealer prices, and the e-way bill made from the app when goods over ₹50,000 move by vehicle.

06

లైసెన్సులుLicences and expiry alerts

Fertilizer, pesticide and seed licences in one place, with a warning before they expire, along with product expiry dates.

07

మందు ఫోటో → వాడకంProduct photo to use and dose

Photograph a pack to hear what it is for, its benefits and the dose per acre, in Telugu, to explain to the farmer.

08

పంట తెగులు సహాయంCrop disease help and training

Photograph a sick leaf for likely causes and what to use, plus short lessons on safe pesticide and fertilizer use.
